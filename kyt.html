Why monitoring matters now

Financial crime no longer looks like one unusual transaction.

The activity that matters is often distributed across time, accounts and relationships. Monitoring must reveal the pattern—not merely inspect each payment in isolation.

01

Faster movement

Funds can be layered across accounts, products and jurisdictions before a manual review begins.

02

Distributed networks

Mule accounts are designed to appear ordinary individually. Their relationships expose the risk.

03

Higher scrutiny

Teams need to show that monitoring is risk-based, reviewed regularly and supported by explainable decisions.

Context: INTERPOL Global Financial Fraud Assessment and FATF risk-based banking guidance.
